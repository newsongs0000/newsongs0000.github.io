# Background pictures

The `ChapterN-bg.jpg` files are the movements' Image backgrounds (`background` in `work.js`; see **Background** in
the README). Each is a public-domain picture from Wikimedia Commons, cropped to 3:2 around its subject and saved
1024 px wide (960 px for Chapter5, whose source is smaller); the backgrounds are shown blurred. The source page has
the full-size original and its license details.

The booklet of the LP set (RCA Victor LD 6091) is illustrated with Luca Signorelli's frescoes of the Last Judgment
in the San Brizio Chapel of Orvieto Cathedral, so movements I–IV use frescoes from that chapel.
