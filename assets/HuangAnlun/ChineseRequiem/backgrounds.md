# Background pictures

## Image set 1: Christian paintings

The `ChapterN-bg.jpg` files are the movements' Image backgrounds (`background` in `work.js`; see **Background** in
the README): Christian art made in China, from the Tang dynasty to the 1930s, all in the public domain. Xu Jihua's
copyright expired in China before 1996, so his paintings are in the public domain in the United States too. Each is
cropped to 3:2 around its subject and saved 1024 px wide (the backgrounds are shown blurred).
