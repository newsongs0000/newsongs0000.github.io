# Bilingual lyrics columns: 40% original, 60% translation

## Ask

English lyrics usually take more space, change the left lyrics panel to take 40% width, the right lyrics panel take
the rest 60% width

## Answer

The bilingual lyrics panel is now split 40% (Chinese, left) / 60% (English, right).

- `common.css`
  - New variable `--lyrics-original-width: 40%` at the top, next to the other lyrics colors. Change it there to move
    the split. The translation column gets the rest.
  - The column grid (`grid-template-columns: var(--lyrics-original-width) 1fr`) and the divider line
    (`left: var(--lyrics-original-width)`) both use it, so they stay together.
  - In bilingual mode the side padding moved from the row to the two cells (24px on each side of the divider). This
    puts the column boundary on the divider instead of off it by the row's padding.
- `README.md`: the layout diagram is redrawn at about 40/60, the "Translation" bullet gives the widths, and the new
  variable is listed under the settings changed in the source.

Movements without translations are unchanged: one full-width column with the usual padding.
