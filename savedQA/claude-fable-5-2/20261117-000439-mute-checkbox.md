# Mute check box in the lyrics panel

- Session: 2026-11-17 00:04
- Model: claude-fable-5-2

## Ask

> Add a checkbox to the lyrics panel at left bottom, when it is checked, mute the audio, but lyrics are still played
> as usual

## Response

Added an **Audio: ☐ Mute** row to the lyrics panel at the bottom of the left pane, under the
**Show: ☑ Original ☑ Translation** row.

When it is checked, the audio is silenced but keeps playing, so everything that follows the audio position works as
before: the lyrics highlight, the progress bar, the time display, the `t` URL parameter and timing recording.

### Behavior

- Checked: `audio.muted = true`. Unchecked: sound comes back at once.
- It stays on from one movement to the next (Prev / Next, clicking the tree, auto-advance at the end).
- It is not saved: reloading the page unchecks it, like the Show check boxes.
- The box gives up focus after a click, so Space and the arrow keys keep controlling playback and lyrics.

### Changes

| File | Change |

### Verification

Tested in headless Chrome against a local HTTP server:

- Clicking the box mutes and unmutes the audio; focus returns to the page, and Space still pauses.
- Chinese Requiem chapter 1, played from 0:08 for 13.5 s, muted and then unmuted: both runs moved the highlight from
  line 2 ("求主") to line 4 ("永远安息，") and showed the same progress bar and time.
- Going to the next movement keeps the mute.

Not tested in Firefox or Safari.
